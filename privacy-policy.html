Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how PDFtoPDF.in (“we”, “our” or “the site”) may handle information when you visit the website. Update this policy whenever the site's technology, analytics, advertising or data practices change.

Files you select

Supported browser-based tools process selected files locally in your browser. The current site is designed not to upload those files to our application server for those supported actions. Your browser may download a result to your device. Do not use a tool for confidential material unless you understand its current behavior and are comfortable with it.

Technical information

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, referrer and request time in server logs for security, reliability and abuse prevention.

Analytics

We may add Google Analytics or another analytics service in the future. If analytics is enabled, it may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits and interactions. This policy and any consent controls should be updated to accurately describe the service actually installed.

Advertising and cookies

We may apply for advertising services such as Google AdSense. If ads are enabled, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own policies and the applicable consent settings. No advertising service should be considered active unless it has actually been installed.

Third-party services

The site may load software libraries and fonts from third-party CDNs. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser requests their resources. Check their policies for details.

Children

The site is intended as a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. Do not submit personal or sensitive information through any feature that requests it.

Your choices and contact

You can control cookies through your browser settings. If analytics, advertising or a contact channel is introduced, this section should identify the actual provider and explain how to contact the site operator. Before launch, publish a monitored contact email or other reliable contact method here.

Changes

We may revise this policy as the website changes. The date at the top should be updated when material changes are made.
